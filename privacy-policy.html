PicScale – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026
Developer: Desrein Studios (Anuj Budhwar), India
Contact: anujbudhwar455@gmail.com
App: PicScale – Photo & Video Resizer (Android, package com.desrein.picscale)

This policy explains what information PicScale handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • Your photos and videos are processed entirely on your device. PicScale never uploads them to us or to anyone else.
  • PicScale has no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not run any server that receives your data.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.
  • Premium purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

2. Photos, videos and files

When you select photos or videos (through the Android photo picker, the camera, or drag-and-drop), PicScale reads them only to resize, compress or convert them on your device. Results are saved to your device (Pictures/PicScale, Movies/PicScale, Downloads/PicScale, or a folder you choose). Files are never transmitted off your device by PicScale.

EXIF metadata: By default, processed images do not keep the original photo's metadata. If you turn on the Premium "Keep EXIF data" option, camera details, date/time and GPS location stored in the original photo are copied into the new file on your device. Keep this in mind before sharing those files.

PicScale stores a few settings locally on your device (for example, your output folder, the "Keep EXIF" setting, lifetime counters for images processed and space saved, and whether Premium is active). These stay on your device, are excluded from cloud backup, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Permissions

  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): needed to save results into shared folders. Not requested on Android 10+.
  • Internet / network state: used only by Google's ad and billing libraries.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google AdMob for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention (see section 4).
  • Billing: lets Google Play process in-app purchases.

PicScale does not request camera, location, contacts, microphone or phone permissions. Taking a photo opens your device's own camera app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner and occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate general location);
  • advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID);
  • product interactions such as app launches, ad taps and ad video views;
  • diagnostic information such as app performance and SDK errors.

This data is encrypted in transit. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, PicScale shows Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services – https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google Privacy Policy – https://policies.google.com/privacy

Premium users do not see ads, and no ad requests are made for them.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

PicScale Premium is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy. PicScale only receives from Google Play a confirmation of which product your Google account owns, which it checks on your device to unlock Premium. We do not collect or store your name, email or payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

6. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect your photos, videos, contacts, precise location or files.
  • We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting services.

7. Children

PicScale is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your personal data on our servers. Data on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google for ads is handled under Google's policies; you can control it through your Google account, Android ad settings and the in-app ad privacy choices.

9. Security

Your files never leave your device through PicScale. Communication between the Google SDKs and Google's servers uses encryption (TLS).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
